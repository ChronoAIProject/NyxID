# NyxID 1.0.5 App Store assets and release notes

The proposed public release notes are in en-US/whats-new.txt. The feature comparison against the published 1.0.4 listing and its documented release is in 1.0.4-to-1.0.5.md.

The final pack contains 24 device screenshots: five large iPhone, five medium iPhone, five landscape iPad, six Duo outer-display and three native Watch images. The original four stories remain in order: An idea. A conversation.; Talk it through.; Connect to your tools.; and Work together with Crews. Page five adds Talk to your agent by voice. Duo adds a sixth page, Do more with Duo., showing the app's Full screen workspace on the outer display.

The original charcoal and purple branding, continuous digital wave and metal device frames match across the series. The fifth voice page includes a close-up of the genuine Listening call controls. Three refreshed Watch promotional cards show choosing an agent, dictating a question, and a real question and reply together.

Full native alternatives, the actual Watch icon, Header/Search Results artwork and continuous series previews are included. The header uses “NOW WITH NYXBOT”, “Give your AI more to work with.” and “Chat, talk, and connect your tools.”, with iPhone, iPad, Duo and Apple Watch. The native Watch screenshot slot uses the three 416 × 496 images; the larger cards are promotional artwork. macOS UI and Duo inner-display captures are excluded.

The README and manifest preserve actual capture provenance: iPhone/iPad/Duo from the full build 9 app, Watch from the unchanged build 11 companion source in a Debug simulator capture build, and target TestFlight build 11. Real example conversations and a three-agent Crew are used. No product UI source was changed for capture. The later QR review is not shown by these listing screenshots.

Public notes and listing changes remain drafts. Preparing the pack does not edit App Store Connect or submit the app for review.

Medium iPhone cards 02–05 and their plain alternatives are resized from the matching large-iPhone images to 1206 × 2622; card 01 retains its original native medium capture.

App Review instructions for version 1.0.5 (11) are in app-review-notes.txt. Paste them into App Store Connect’s App Review Information > Notes; enter dedicated review credentials separately in the private Sign-in Information fields. Review-account password sign-in and feature access must be verified separately; a credit balance alone does not establish them.

The pack also includes verification/app-review-voice-and-watch.png, a visual reference combining the live voice screen with enlarged native Watch Agents, Tap to speak, and Conversation states.
