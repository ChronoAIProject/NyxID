# NyxID 1.0.4 to 1.0.5

This comparison uses the live U.S. App Store 1.0.4 release notes, published 13 November 2026, the documented 1.0.4 (58) submission, subsequent mobile source history and the frozen 1.0.5 (11) release. Apple's public listing does not expose the source hash of the shipped 1.0.4 binary, so this is a feature comparison against the documented public baseline.

| Area | Public 1.0.4 baseline | 1.0.5 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Devices | iPhone and adaptive iPad layouts, including rotation and multitasking | Adds iPhone Duo layouts for both displays and the quick-access rail; refines ordinary iPhone and iPad layouts, sidebar and window behavior |
| Apple Watch | No companion described in the 1.0.4 listing | Basic companion for unread activity, agent conversations with dictation, and eligible approval decisions; setup through the iPhone app |
| Notifications | No notification update described in the 1.0.4 release notes | Feed refinements, notification search, clear unread counts outside the bell, simpler filters, one page title and Mark all as read in the options menu |
| Composer and drafts | In-composer dictation and general composer improvements | One-line idle composer that expands on focus or non-whitespace input, with preserved drafts and chat state |
| Attachments | Improved attachment progress presentation | Image/PDF preview strip, horizontal browsing, individual failures, interrupted-upload recovery and clearer recovery controls |
| QR sign-in | QR scanning already existed | More compact review, grouped device/browser/request/network information, More details inside the card, visible warnings and explicit approval/rejection actions |
| Sessions and sign-in | Existing account/session controls and improved sign-in presentation | Better recovery after backgrounding or temporary storage/network failures, clearer inline sign-in errors and improved sign-out handling |
| Keyboard and accessibility | General chat and voice layout improvements | Better reachability of forms and navigation, wrapping sign-in errors and long labels, and improved search-field alignment |
| Languages | English, Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese across supported settings and selected controls | Broader translation coverage and follow-up corrections, including voice labels and the final QR review |
| Voice | Compact panel, thinner waveform, loudspeaker routing, searchable voice selection and improved recovery already advertised | Further background-call, dictation-language, permission and recovery refinements |

The public What's New draft is in en-US/whats-new.txt. It intentionally describes iPad, voice and languages as improvements rather than first-time additions.

## Staging QR rollout

The mobile binary contains production-to-staging QR account-sign-in support. Staging requires supporting backends, a configured production verification key on staging, enabled production account access, active personal accounts with the same verified email in both environments and MFA where configured. The build 11 release recorded production at 0.78.0 and staging at 0.79.0; an end-to-end staging approval was not verified. The public What's New draft therefore describes the QR review changes without advertising staging sign-in as generally available.

## Screenshot scope

The refreshed App Store set contains five large-iPhone images, five landscape iPad images, six iPhone Duo outer-display images, five medium-iPhone images and three native Apple Watch images. The original four stories are An idea. A conversation.; Talk it through.; Connect to your tools.; and Work together with Crews. Page five adds Talk to your agent by voice. Duo page six, Do more with Duo., shows the app’s Full screen workspace on its outer display. The refreshed Watch series shows agents, dictation and a real question and reply together. The original NyxID branding is consistent across the set, with a continuous violet wave and more realistic device frames.

Medium iPhone cards 02–05 and their plain alternatives are resized from the matching large-iPhone images to 1206 × 2622. Card 01 retains its original native medium capture.

The pack includes full native alternatives, three branded Watch promotional cards, the actual Watch app icon, Header and Search Results artwork, and continuous series previews. Use the three 416 × 496 native images in the Apple Watch screenshot slot; the larger branded Watch cards are promotional artwork. macOS UI and Duo inner-display images are excluded.

The iPhone, iPad and Duo screens are genuine captures from the full 1.0.5 (9) app and the clean listing workspace, with real assistant and three-agent Crew replies. Those workspace features carry into target build 11. The native Watch screens come from the unchanged 1.0.5 (11) companion source, built in Debug for simulator capture. No product UI source was changed for these captures. None of the listing screenshots depicts the later QR review.
