Bring your AI assistants, connected tools, and access controls together with NyxID.

Chat with NyxBot, talk through a task, and keep your conversations and account controls close at hand. NyxID gives you a mobile workspace for working with AI while managing the services and permissions behind it.

CHAT AND TALK
Start a conversation with NyxBot or one of your agents. Type a message, dictate into the composer, or open a live voice conversation. Choose hands-free or hold-to-talk mode, select a voice for your next call, and return to your conversation history when you need it.

CONNECT YOUR TOOLS
Browse connectors and skills, manage supported connections, and give your assistants access to the services you choose. Some setup and advanced controls open the NyxID website.

WORK WITH AGENTS AND CREWS
Keep individual conversations and crew chats in one workspace. Follow responses, review requests, and pick up your work across your available agents.

STAY IN CONTROL
Review access requests before approving or denying them. Manage account security and active sessions, and use the account controls to sign out or request permanent account deletion.

MAKE IT YOURS
Adjust display and chat preferences. Choose English, Simplified Chinese, or Traditional Chinese for supported settings screens. Messages stay in their original language, and dictation language is configured separately.

DESIGNED FOR IPHONE AND IPAD
Use a compact workspace on iPhone and an adaptive layout on iPad, including supported rotation and multitasking.

A NyxID account and an internet connection are required. Available features depend on your account, enabled services, and provider access. AI model and connected-service usage may incur charges.
