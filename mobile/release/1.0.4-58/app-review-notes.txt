NyxID 1.0.4 (58)

NyxID is an AI workspace with account and service access controls. The update expands the previous mobile-authenticator presentation with conversations, voice, connected tools, agents, and account settings. A NyxID account is required.

Sign-in details
Enter the dedicated review account in App Store Connect's separate Sign-in Information fields. Use an account that can sign in without assistance from a team member. Review credentials are not included in this source-controlled file.

Main review flow
1. Sign in with the supplied email and password using the email fields on the sign-in screen.
2. Open a new chat with NyxBot and enter a short request, such as “Draft a three-step project checklist.”
3. To review voice, use Open voice call beside the message composer. Allow microphone access when prompted. The account must have an available voice source and enough credit/provider access. Switch between Hands-free and Hold to talk using the voice panel's controls. End the call using its close/end control.
4. During a voice call, open Chat options → Voice preferences. You can also use Settings → Voice to choose a voice for the next call. Playable voice samples are not included.
5. Open Settings → Language to choose English, Simplified Chinese, or Traditional Chinese for supported settings screens. Messages retain their original language. Dictation language is configured separately under Voice settings.
6. Open Settings → Account & Security to review supported account-security and active-session controls. The supplied review account must not require a device controlled by the developer to complete sign-in.

Account deletion
Use the separate disposable deletion-test account supplied in Sign-in Information or review attachments. Account deletion is available under Settings → Delete Account and requires confirmation. The app signs the account out after deletion is accepted. Cleanup may continue until verified. Do not use the primary review account for this destructive test if it is needed to continue the review.

Permissions
Microphone and speech-recognition access support live voice and dictation. Camera and photo access support QR scanning and user-selected attachments. These features are initiated by the user. Network access is required for sign-in and AI services.

AI content and support
Completed AI replies provide a Report control. Group/crew features depend on account access. Support is available from Settings → Contact support at contact@chrono-ai.fun.

Some connection setup and advanced settings intentionally open the NyxID website. Features depend on account configuration and server availability. Ensure the review account is provisioned before submission.
