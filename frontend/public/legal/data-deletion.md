# Data Deletion Instructions

This page explains how to delete the data NyxID holds about you, including data NyxID receives from Meta products such as WhatsApp and Facebook Login. Our [Privacy Policy](https://nyxid.ai/privacy) describes what we collect and how long we keep it.

## Delete your NyxID account

Deleting your account removes your data from NyxID, including your connected messaging channels (such as WhatsApp) and their stored credentials, your service connections, and your messaging metadata.

1. Sign in at [nyxid.ai](https://nyxid.ai).
2. Open **Settings → Security**.
3. Choose **Delete Account** and confirm.

Account access ends as soon as the request is accepted, and data removal continues in the background. Some audit and billing records are kept for integrity and safety, as described in section 6 of the Privacy Policy.

## Disconnect a messaging channel

To stop NyxID from sending and receiving messages for one channel, such as a WhatsApp number, without deleting your account:

1. Open **Channel Bots** and select the channel.
2. Choose **Delete** and confirm.

Messaging through that channel stops immediately and its routes are deactivated. For WhatsApp numbers connected with **Connect with Meta**, NyxID also attempts to remove its webhook override from the WhatsApp Business Account when no other active channel connected through NyxID uses that account; this cleanup can fail. Deleting a channel does not revoke the access you granted NyxID in Meta (see below). The deactivated channel's encrypted credentials are removed when you delete your account or ask us to remove them.

## Remove NyxID's access in Meta

You can revoke NyxID's access from Meta at any time. In your Meta business settings, remove NyxID from the apps and integrations connected to your business. NyxID can then no longer access your WhatsApp Business Account.

## If you messaged a business that uses NyxID

When a business uses NyxID for its WhatsApp or other messaging channel, NyxID processes your phone number or platform ID, profile name, and message metadata on that business's behalf. Contact the business, or email us as described below, to request deletion.

## Request deletion by email

Email [contact@chrono-ai.fun](mailto:contact@chrono-ai.fun) with the subject "Data deletion request". Send it from the email address on your NyxID account, or include the phone number or platform ID you used to message a business. We verify the request, tell you the expected timeline when we acknowledge it, and confirm when deletion is complete.
